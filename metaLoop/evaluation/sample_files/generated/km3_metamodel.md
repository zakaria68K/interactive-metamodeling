The system shall manage collections of related design entities, where each collection acts as a container for various elements that define the structure and behavior of a subject of interest. Each entity within a collection must have a distinct identifier to support clear differentiation and referencing. Entities that represent abstract or concrete concepts must be capable of containing multiple features that describe their properties or relationships to other entities. These features are categorized into those representing simple data attributes and those representing associations with other entities.

Attributes within an entity shall be typed, linking to a defined kind of data, either primitive or complex, ensuring the identification of the nature of the values they hold. Associations serve to model directed links between entities, specifying both the origin and destination to capture relationships within the system’s domain. Entities may define operations that encapsulate behaviors or actions applicable to them; these operations include inputs or outputs described with named, typed elements to clarify their usage.

The system must handle enumerations, which are special entities that define a fixed set of permissible literal values to constrain certain types of attributes. Each enumeration consists of one or more distinct literals that represent allowable options. Collections can be nested, allowing one collection to be contained within another to build complex hierarchies of entities.

Elements managed by the system retain location information to facilitate their identification within source materials or models, supporting traceability and error reporting. Furthermore, the system shall enforce consistency such that every entity and its corresponding features are properly contained within a single collection. When defining features or operations, their associated types must be valid entities within the model or predefined data types to maintain semantic correctness.

Overall, the system shall provide comprehensive support for organizing, referencing, and structuring entities, their attributes, relationships, and behaviors, while ensuring clear ownership and hierarchical organization within collections, enabling the accurate modeling of complex domains through an extensible and well-typed framework.

Author: Yuval Noah Harari wrote "Sapiens" (Category: History) and "Homo Deus" (Category: Futurism).
The Library holds 6 Copies of "Sapiens" and 3 Copies of "Homo Deus".
Both Books are cataloged in the Central and Eastside Library branches.
A third Book "21 Lessons" by the same Author has 2 Copies at Central Library.

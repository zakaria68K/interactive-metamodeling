The system shall manage collections of information feeds, each uniquely identified and containing a set of individual entries representing discrete pieces of content. Each collection must possess descriptive attributes including a title, a link to its origin, and a textual summary, which provide an overview of its purpose and content. Optionally, the system allows each collection to include ownership details such as the name and email of the managing entity. To enhance user experience, the system shall support associating one visual representation per collection, characterized by a title, a link destination, and an image URI.

Within each collection, multiple entries are maintained, each encapsulating a single unit of content. Each entry shall include a title, a link to the full content, and a descriptive summary. Entries may optionally contain publication dates, authorship information, and unique identifiers ensuring distinctness across entries. The system shall support associating one or more groups to each entry, enabling thematic classification. 

For each content entry, the system must manage optional downloadable resources, characterized by a URI, length in bytes, and media type, accommodating various formats such as audio or video. Additionally, a mechanism shall exist to store references to external origins of entries, including the original title and link, which support tracking source attribution. 

Communication enhancements are supported whereby the system facilitates maintaining network-based subscription services. These services are identified by domain and port information, specifying the procedural protocol and facilitating notifications about content updates. The system must also support receiving textual input from users or external sources, including necessary parameters such as title, description, name, and link, to support interactive or submission features.

Each collection may further categorize its content via classification labels to enable efficient filtering and discovery. These labels are named and may include a hierarchical or organizational structure. The system shall ensure that within any collection, each category is distinctly identifiable.

Overall, the system shall coordinate the relationships between collections, entries, media resources, classifications, communication protocols, and external references ensuring consistent, accurate, and accessible data management conforming to standardized content syndication practices.

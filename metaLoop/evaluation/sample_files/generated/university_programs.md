Programme Overview – BSc Software Engineering

The BSc Software Engineering degree is run by the CS faculty and spans eight terms. Laura Diaz is currently enrolled and taking four classes in the autumn 2026 term.

Alan Grant, a faculty member in the department, coordinates the degree and also teaches Software Design (CS401), which meets in room D102. Laura submitted a project proposal for CS401 and received a B from Grant.

Students must complete core classes across several faculties — including Mathematics — to qualify for the degree. Academic progress is tracked through term-by-term registrations and accumulated marks.
